# TOHFA Launch Readiness Review: Decision Register

**Story Reference:** S-53  
**Audience:** Client Leadership, Product Steering Committee, Lead Architect  
**Purpose:** Comprehensive, evidenced audit of every assumption and design decision made on the client's behalf during Track 1 development, including resolution status for all 13 open contradictions from `docs/rules.md`.

## 1. Architectural & Operational Decisions Taken

| # | Domain / Area | Decision Taken | Assumption / Rationale | Cost to Alter in Phase 2 | Authority Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **D-01** | **Monetary Arithmetic** | All money calculations use integer minor units (paise/cents) wrapped in a dedicated `Money` value object (`packages/shared-types`). Float arithmetic is strictly prohibited in financial tables. | Prevents IEEE 754 precision drift in wallet balances, cart totals, GST, and payouts. | High (Database migration & schema rebuild if float is required). | Lead Architect / CFO |
| **D-02** | **Farm Anonymity (BR-16)** | Customer catalog API and mobile app operate on aggregated warehouse inventory. Customer payloads NEVER receive farmer identity, farm name, or harvest coordinates. | Enforces non-negotiable PRD farm anonymity to protect supply lines and prevent direct off-platform circumvention. | Very High (Requires redesigning the entire catalog pipeline). | Client CEO / Business Lead |
| **D-03** | **Fair-Price Ceiling (BR-07)** | Hard rejection at produce listing creation if price exceeds the commodity's fair-price ceiling (`PRICE_ABOVE_CEILING` 422). | Prevents speculative price inflation and guarantees buyer fairness. | Low (Config flag to make warning instead of hard error). | Head of Agri Procurement |
| **D-04** | **Counter-Offer State Machine (BR-10, BR-11)** | 24-hour response window with automated lapse-on-expiry, strict 3-round cap, and automatic acceptance when farmer meets warehouse ceiling. | Prevents zombie negotiations and locks terms before Purchase Order creation. | Medium (State machine parameter configuration). | Operations Director |
| **D-05** | **Cart Reservation Lock (BR-22)** | 24-hour stock lock against the warehouse Online allocation bucket with automated release job. | Prevents double-selling inventory while customers complete checkout or wallet top-up. | Low (Adjust `CART_LOCK_DURATION_HOURS` config). | Head of Digital Commerce |
| **D-06** | **Wallet-First Settlement (BR-17)** | Checkout only succeeds if user wallet has sufficient funds. When insufficient, returns 422 `WALLET_INSUFFICIENT` with exact shortfall amount for 1-tap Razorpay top-up recovery. | Removes cash-on-delivery payment friction and eliminates courier reconciliation overhead. | Medium (Adding COD requires courier reconciliation service). | Finance Lead / CEO |
| **D-07** | **Fulfilment Handover OTP (BR-20)** | 4-digit numeric OTP generated on order packaging, held exclusively by customer mobile app, and verified by warehouse admin at physical handover. | Guarantees physical proof of delivery without requiring mobile network coverage at the customer device during handover. | Low (Can be augmented with QR scanning in Phase 2). | Warehouse Logistics Lead |
| **D-08** | **Dual Payout Approval (BR-28, BR-29)** | Farmer payout dues require two distinct admin approvals (Finance Admin + Super Admin) before bank disbursement. | Safeguards platform treasury against single-operator fraud or mistaken disbursements. | Low (Threshold configuration in RBAC). | Compliance Officer |
| **D-09** | **Aadhaar Privacy (BR-33, BR-32)** | Aadhaar numbers encrypted via AES-256 at rest, masked in logs (`+9198****3210` / `****-****-1234`), and accompanied by prominent in-app disclosure. | Fulfills UIDAI regulations and Google Play Data Safety compliance. | Zero (Mandatory legal compliance). | Legal Counsel |
| **D-10** | **Mobile Baselines (S-51)** | Authoritative minimum support baselines locked to Android 8.0 (API 26) and iOS 15.1 (raised from 13.0, the React Native 0.88 minimum) with Hermes JS engine. | Maximizes addressable farmer market across rural Tamil Nadu on entry-level 2GB RAM devices. | Medium (Raising baseline drops older handsets). | Mobile Engineering Lead |
